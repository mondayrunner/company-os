# Compass

What steers the advice. Not a checklist; a direction. Half-filled is fine.

## Anti-vision

A Tuesday in five years where every decision goes through three layers of a client's management and we bill by the hour for waiting.

- **Craft.** Building things ourselves, well.
- **Autonomy.** Choosing what we take on and with whom.

## Vision

A Tuesday where one of us teaches a client's team to build their own tools, and the other one improves the platform that runs the subscriptions.

## This year

- Ten subscriptions.
- No project quotes below the day rate.

## Boundaries

- No hourly work.
- No white-label work under another agency's name.
